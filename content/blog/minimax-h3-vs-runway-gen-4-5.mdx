> **Quick answer:** Choose MiniMax H3 when synchronized audio, text/image/video/audio references, first-and-last-frame control, direct 2K selection, or open-weight experimentation defines the job. Choose Runway Gen-4.5 when Runway's mature creative workspace, Sessions, Workflows, editor-adjacent tools, or strong text/image-to-video motion workflow matters more. Test the same deliverable because these products do not expose identical controls.

The useful **MiniMax H3 vs Runway Gen-4.5** comparison is not a contest between two showcase reels. It is a comparison between an omni-reference audio-video model workflow and a broad creative platform whose current first-party generator emphasizes text-to-video and image-to-video. Runway can also expose other models, audio tools, editing models, Agents, and node-based Workflows, so name **Gen-4.5** whenever the model—not the whole Runway platform—is being scored.

## Version and access status

Runway introduced Gen-4.5 in December 2025 and currently describes it as its advanced general video model. The official Gen-4.5 guide documents Text to Video and Image to Video, 2–10 second duration, 24/25 FPS, and generated dimensions centered on a 720P tier. Runway also offers Aleph for video editing and other third-party models, but those outputs should not be labeled Gen-4.5.

This page was verified on August 7, 2026. A future Runway model should be added to this version-neutral decision framework only after its official guide and actual access route are available.

## Input and creative control

H3 is structurally attractive when the brief contains several kinds of evidence: a product image for identity, a motion clip for performance, an audio file for voice or rhythm, and a text instruction defining what each reference contributes. Its first-and-last-frame mode is also useful when endpoints matter.

Gen-4.5 currently gives a simpler base choice. Text-to-video describes visuals and motion; image-to-video uses an initial image and a motion-focused prompt. Runway says additional control modes will expand over time, but comparisons must use capabilities available in the tested interface—not announcement language about future support.

That makes the fair test asymmetric. First compare both with text only. Then compare H3 multimodal reference against the closest complete Runway workflow, clearly labeling any additional model, App, or editing step.

## Motion, physics, and camera direction

Runway positions Gen-4.5 around motion quality, prompt adherence, temporal consistency, and physical behavior. Those are vendor claims that justify testing, not a universal score. H3 should be tested on the same action sequence with the same shot length and aspect ratio.

Use a prompt containing three timed beats rather than vague cinematic adjectives. Score whether the subject completes each action, maintains form during contact, and follows the camera instruction. Keep retry counts equal. A beautiful first frame cannot compensate for a failed required action.

## Native audio versus a broader audio toolchain

H3 can generate dialogue, ambience, effects, and music direction together with the video. This is valuable when sound timing is part of the performance. Runway provides audio tools, including speech and workflow nodes, but Gen-4.5 itself should not be credited with a separately generated track.

Compare final deliverables in two ways. First, evaluate the raw generated clip. Second, evaluate the complete workflow result and include every audio step in time and cost. This distinguishes model capability from platform capability without dismissing either.

## Resolution and finishing

H3 offers direct 2K in the documented V2 route. Runway's current Gen-4.5 guide documents 720P generation and the platform offers upscale and continuation tools. A fair production comparison records both the generated resolution and final delivered resolution. Upscaling does not retroactively make the base generation native at the delivery size.

For social drafts, 720P or 768P may be enough. For text, product detail, cropping, or large-screen delivery, test the entire finishing pipeline rather than relying on the resolution label.

## Cost comparison

Runway documents Gen-4.5 at 12 Runway credits per generated second. Its plan credits, Explore Mode access, rollover rules, and additional tools change effective cost. On minimaxh3.pro, H3 uses 25 credits per second at 768P or 40 credits per second at 2K before billable reference adjustments. These credit units are unrelated currencies and should never be compared numerically.

Calculate dollars spent on all attempts divided by accepted shots. Include upscaling, audio, editing, expired subscription credits, and operator time. The [MiniMax H3 cost guide](/blog/minimax-h3-cost) explains the H3 side; verify Runway's live pricing before publication or purchase.

## Same-prompt test

Use this common text-only baseline:

> Eight-second continuous product shot in a rain-dark studio. A brushed-metal travel speaker sits on wet black stone. The camera begins at table height, arcs slowly clockwise, then pushes toward the volume dial as one hand turns it exactly once. Water droplets vibrate subtly from a deep bass note. Preserve speaker geometry, one hand only, realistic contact, controlled reflections, no cuts, no extra objects.

Run four outputs per model at the closest common aspect ratio. Score action completion, hand contact, geometry, camera path, reflections, audio, resolution, latency, and actual cost. Then run a separate reference test and disclose that the input sets differ.

## Who should choose each?

Choose MiniMax H3 for reference-rich audio-video direction, direct 2K, endpoint control, or H3-specific local research. Choose Runway Gen-4.5 when the surrounding Runway platform, fast text/image iteration, team asset organization, Workflows, or adjacent editing capabilities provide more production value. A team can use both if accepted-shot evidence supports the added operational complexity.

## FAQ

### Is MiniMax H3 better than Runway Gen-4.5?

Not universally. H3 exposes broader reference modalities and native audio, while Runway provides a mature creative platform around Gen-4.5. The required deliverable determines which difference matters.

### Does Runway Gen-4.5 generate native audio?

Runway offers audio tools, but the current official Gen-4.5 generation guide describes text/image-to-video. Label separately generated audio as a platform workflow, not a Gen-4.5 native output.

### Which supports higher generated resolution?

H3 documents direct 2K selection. The current Gen-4.5 guide documents 720P-oriented outputs, with finishing tools available in the broader Runway platform.

### Which is cheaper?

Compare actual dollars per accepted shot. Credit units, subscription utilization, retries, upscaling, and audio steps differ.
