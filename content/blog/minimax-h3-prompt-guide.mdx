This library contains **20 copy-and-paste MiniMax H3 prompt examples**: ten text-to-video shots, five first-and-last-frame animations, and five multimodal directions. Each includes a scene, duration, resolution and mode. These are starting directions, not measured performance results or guarantees.

## How to use these prompts

Copy a prompt into the [Playground](/#playground), then set the stated duration and resolution in the controls; words such as ?2K? inside a prompt do not change those settings. Select the ratio described in text-to-video prompts. Frame-based outputs follow the uploaded images. Start at 768P if you want a cheaper test before a 2K finish.

For frame examples, supply the described first frame and, where specified, a compatible last frame. For multimodal examples, add the named assets in order: Image 1, Image 2, Video 1 or Audio 1. Audio-reference examples also require an image or video in this workspace. Use assets you have permission to upload. Review the full credit estimate before submitting.

## Text-to-video prompt examples

### 1. Watch macro reveal

**Duration:** 4 seconds ? **Resolution:** 768P ? **Mode:** text-to-video

```text
Four-second macro product shot in 16:9. A brushed-steel watch rests on black stone. A narrow warm light crosses the dial while the camera makes a slow clockwise orbit. Realistic metal reflections, shallow focus, one quiet mechanical click and soft room tone, no music. Keep the case geometry, dial and hands stable.
```

### 2. Fragrance commercial

**Duration:** 10 seconds ? **Resolution:** 2K ? **Mode:** text-to-video

```text
Ten-second 16:9 fragrance commercial. A clear perfume bottle stands on wet black stone. From zero to three seconds, show condensation in macro. Slowly orbit until seven seconds, then pull back into a thin veil of mist. Burgundy shadows and restrained gold reflections. Hear droplets and one low cello note. Keep the bottle shape and label placement stable; no added text.
```

### 3. Vertical coffee ritual

**Duration:** 6 seconds ? **Resolution:** 768P ? **Mode:** text-to-video

```text
Six-second vertical 9:16 cafe scene. An adult barista pours steamed milk into a ceramic cup, creating one simple heart. Begin above the cup and gently lower to rim height. Warm morning window light, realistic liquid motion. Synchronized pouring, a soft ceramic tap and quiet cafe ambience. One continuous take, no subtitles or sudden cuts.
```

### 4. Electric car at blue hour

**Duration:** 8 seconds ? **Resolution:** 2K ? **Mode:** text-to-video

```text
Eight-second 16:9 automotive film. A pearl-white electric coupe waits on a misty mountain road. Begin with a close view of its headlamp, which switches on at two seconds. Track beside the front wheel as the car slowly enters a wide bend. Cool blue light and realistic suspension. Hear electric motor tone, tire hiss and wind. Preserve car proportions and paint color.
```

### 5. Rainy city establishing shot

**Duration:** 8 seconds ? **Resolution:** 768P ? **Mode:** text-to-video

```text
Eight-second 21:9 city establishing shot. Rain falls onto an empty narrow street at night, reflecting warm shop windows. The camera moves forward slowly at pedestrian height past one puddle. Natural rain motion, soft distant haze and muted colors. Rain and distant traffic only. One continuous take; no readable signs, crowds or camera shake.
```

### 6. Short spoken product tip

**Duration:** 6 seconds ? **Resolution:** 768P ? **Mode:** text-to-video

```text
Six-second vertical 9:16 social clip. An adult presenter in a bright kitchen holds a plain reusable bottle and says, "I take this on every morning walk." Medium close-up, gentle handheld movement, natural window light. Clear conversational voice with quiet room tone, no background music. Maintain accurate lip synchronization and consistent bottle shape; no captions.
```

### 7. Forest wildlife insert

**Duration:** 5 seconds ? **Resolution:** 2K ? **Mode:** text-to-video

```text
Five-second 16:9 wildlife insert. A small fox pauses beside a mossy log, turns toward a faint sound, then walks out of frame. Low static camera, soft early-morning light and restrained depth of field. Hear leaves, distant birds and a light breeze. Preserve realistic fur, anatomy and ground contact; no slow motion or cuts.
```

### 8. Science-fiction corridor

**Duration:** 10 seconds ? **Resolution:** 768P ? **Mode:** text-to-video

```text
Ten-second 21:9 science-fiction scene. An adult engineer enters a dim spacecraft corridor, stops at a glowing panel at four seconds, then watches a door open at eight seconds. Slow tracking camera behind the shoulder, cool practical lights and worn metal surfaces. Footsteps, a low ventilation hum and a soft door mechanism. Keep the corridor layout and costume consistent.
```

### 9. Ceramic studio loop study

**Duration:** 4 seconds ? **Resolution:** 768P ? **Mode:** text-to-video

```text
Four-second 1:1 studio motion study. A simple ceramic vase rotates slowly on a turntable against a cream background. Fixed camera, diffuse light, natural clay texture and stable shadows. Quiet room tone only. Maintain the vase silhouette and surface details throughout; avoid morphing, extra objects and text. Begin and finish with nearly matching composition for loop editing.
```

### 10. Fashion fabric detail

**Duration:** 8 seconds ? **Resolution:** 2K ? **Mode:** text-to-video

```text
Eight-second vertical 9:16 fashion detail. An adult model wearing an unbranded ivory coat takes one slow step across a sunlit studio. Begin at the sleeve texture and pull back to a waist-up view. Soft neutral palette, realistic fabric folds and gentle motion. Hear fabric movement and one footstep, no speech or music. Keep the coat design and face consistent.
```

## First-and-last-frame prompt examples

### 11. Animate a landscape still

**Duration:** 6 seconds ? **Resolution:** 768P ? **Mode:** first-last-frame

```text
Six-second landscape animation using the supplied first frame. Preserve its mountains, horizon, colors and framing. Move the camera forward very slowly while thin clouds drift and distant grass moves in the wind. Natural depth and realistic lighting. Soft wind and distant birds only. Do not introduce buildings, change the terrain or add a cut.
```

### 12. Storyboard bridge

**Duration:** 8 seconds ? **Resolution:** 2K ? **Mode:** first-last-frame

```text
Eight-second transition between the supplied first and last frames. Begin exactly at the first composition. Move smoothly toward the final composition while the visible subject completes one natural step and turns toward the final viewing direction. Keep lighting and identity consistent. Quiet footsteps and room tone. Resolve on the last frame, with no abrupt cut or unrelated transformation.
```

### 13. Product packaging open

**Duration:** 6 seconds ? **Resolution:** 768P ? **Mode:** first-last-frame

```text
Six-second product animation. The first frame shows the closed box; the last frame shows the same box open with its product visible. Use a fixed front three-quarter camera and slowly lift the lid in one physically plausible motion. Preserve package dimensions, colors and product placement. Soft cardboard movement and quiet room tone. Do not invent hands, redesign the product or add text.
```

### 14. Illustration comes alive

**Duration:** 5 seconds ? **Resolution:** 768P ? **Mode:** first-last-frame

```text
Five-second animation of the supplied opening illustration. Keep its linework, palette and character proportions. The character blinks once, gently tilts its head and returns to the starting pose while the camera remains fixed. Preserve the illustrated style without becoming photorealistic. A light breeze and one soft chime; no dialogue, extra characters or text.
```

### 15. Room daylight transition

**Duration:** 10 seconds ? **Resolution:** 2K ? **Mode:** first-last-frame

```text
Ten-second architectural transition between the supplied morning first frame and evening last frame of the same room. Keep walls, furniture and camera position fixed. Gradually change sunlight direction, shadow length and interior light warmth. No objects appear or disappear. Quiet room tone only. End at the final frame without a cut or geometry drift.
```

## Multimodal reference prompt examples

### 16. Reference-led product orbit

**Duration:** 8 seconds ? **Resolution:** 2K ? **Mode:** multimodal

```text
Eight-second 16:9 product commercial. Use Image 1 for the product design and Image 2 for the studio color palette. Place the product on a matte pedestal and slowly orbit from front to three-quarter view. Preserve exact silhouette, materials and component placement; transfer only the colors from Image 2. Hear subtle room tone and one gentle musical pulse. No extra logos or props.
```

### 17. Character continuity walk

**Duration:** 6 seconds ? **Resolution:** 768P ? **Mode:** multimodal

```text
Six-second 16:9 character shot. Use Image 1 for the adult character identity and Image 2 for clothing. The character walks along a quiet garden path, stops and looks toward the camera. Gentle eye-level tracking, soft afternoon light. Preserve face, hairstyle and wardrobe throughout. Footsteps, leaves and distant birds, no dialogue or music.
```

### 18. Motion-reference dancer

**Duration:** 8 seconds ? **Resolution:** 768P ? **Mode:** multimodal

```text
Eight-second vertical 9:16 dance clip. Use Image 1 for the adult performer identity and Video 1 only for the motion rhythm. Stage the performer in a plain studio with full-body framing and a static camera. Follow the reference movement while preserving the image identity, realistic anatomy and floor contact. Soft percussion matched to the movement. Do not copy the reference background or costume.
```

### 19. Camera-reference interior

**Duration:** 10 seconds ? **Resolution:** 2K ? **Mode:** multimodal

```text
Ten-second 16:9 architectural shot. Use Image 1 for room design and Video 1 only for the camera movement. Recreate the camera pace and path inside the room while keeping its furniture, materials and layout stable. Soft natural daylight and controlled reflections. Quiet interior ambience, no music or speech. Do not import subjects or objects from the video reference.
```

### 20. Voice-reference greeting

**Duration:** 6 seconds ? **Resolution:** 768P ? **Mode:** multimodal

```text
Six-second vertical 9:16 greeting. Use Image 1 for the adult presenter and authorized Audio 1 for voice character only. The presenter smiles and says, "Welcome back. Let us make something useful today." Medium close-up, fixed camera and soft daylight. Match spoken timing to natural lip motion. Preserve face and clothing. Quiet room tone, no captions, music or extra speakers.
```

## Adapt an example to your project

Keep the main action and camera move while changing one variable at a time: product, setting, palette or sound. When you need an exact identity or design, switch to references instead of adding more descriptive adjectives.
