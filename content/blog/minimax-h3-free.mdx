**MiniMax H3 is free to try on minimaxh3.pro with a one-time 100-credit welcome balance.** That funds one four-second 768P output-only generation. It does not fund a 2K generation or unlimited videos. The welcome credits expire after 30 days, and your account balance determines what you can submit.

These are this independent site's current configured terms, checked on October 6, 2026. They do not describe free offers on MiniMax's official platform or another host.

## How many free videos can you make?

The output rate is 25 credits/second at 768P and 40 credits/second at 2K. With only the 100-credit welcome balance:

| Output setting | Credits per video | Videos covered by 100 credits |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 4 seconds, 768P | 100 | 1 |
| 5 seconds, 768P | 125 | 0 |
| 10 seconds, 768P | 250 | 0 |
| 15 seconds, 768P | 375 | 0 |
| 4 seconds, 2K | 160 | 0 |
| 10 seconds, 2K | 400 | 0 |
| 15 seconds, 2K | 600 | 0 |

The table assumes no billable reference inputs. A successful first generation spends the welcome balance even if you decide to discard the clip. A new prompt variation is a new generation. Failed tasks are reconciled according to the credit settlement workflow; check your account ledger before trying again.

## Does the free video have a watermark?

The Playground offers an **AIGC watermark** toggle, which defaults to off. The site does not impose an additional watermark just because welcome credits paid for the request. This describes the visible option in this workspace, not a guarantee about every provider's metadata or labeling.

Select the option before submitting. Follow applicable disclosure rules for your intended use, and inspect the downloaded output. Another H3 host may have different watermark or export terms.

## What is the free duration limit?

The current workspace accepts 4–15 seconds at 768P or 2K. There is no separate free-only duration control. The practical limit is the balance: 100 credits can fund only four seconds at 768P. Selecting five seconds or any 2K output increases the estimate beyond that balance.

For your first free test, choose text-to-video, four seconds and 768P. Use one clear subject, one action and a simple camera move. The [prompt examples library](/blog/minimax-h3-prompt-guide) includes several four-second concepts.

## Can you use references with welcome credits?

Frame and reference workflows follow the same credit rules, but some inputs increase the required reservation. The current calculation includes the first five reference images. Additional images add 10 credits each. A request containing reference video reserves up to 15 input-video seconds in addition to output duration.

A four-second 768P request containing reference video may therefore reserve 475 credits, which the welcome balance cannot cover. Use [credits documentation](/docs/credits-and-pricing) to distinguish the temporary reservation from the final settled charge.

## When do you need to pay?

Buy credits when you need a second completed attempt, a longer clip, a 2K result, billable references, or a new balance after welcome-credit expiry. The welcome grant is one-time; it is not a daily renewal or a subscription allowance.

Choose a monthly plan for repeated use or a one-time pack for occasional projects. Open [Pricing and its credit calculator](/pricing), then check the complete estimate in the [Playground](/#playground) before submitting. The [cost guide](/blog/minimax-h3-cost) explains how retries and finishing affect the cost of a usable result.

## Is the official MiniMax H3 API free too?

Site welcome credits do not transfer to an official MiniMax developer account. Provider API access has its own key, balance and billing. Read the [MiniMax H3 API guide](/blog/minimax-h3-api) for official and hosted access routes.
