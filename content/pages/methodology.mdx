minimaxh3.pro publishes hands-on MiniMax H3 guides: pricing breakdowns, local
deployment notes, prompt structures, and comparisons against other video
models. This page explains how those claims are produced, so you can judge how
much weight to give them.

We are an independent third-party service. We do not receive test access,
review copies, or payment from MiniMax, and nothing on this site is approved by
them before publication.

## What we test first-hand

- **Prompt adherence.** Does the clip contain the subject, action, camera move,
  and sound the prompt asked for, or only part of it?
- **Motion coherence.** Do limbs, fabric, hair, wheels, and liquids move
  plausibly across the full clip, including the last second?
- **Identity retention.** When a reference image or video is supplied, does the
  face, clothing, and object stay consistent through the shot?
- **On-screen text.** Do captions, logos, and UI labels stay legible and
  unchanged instead of drifting into nonsense?
- **Audio.** Is dialogue or ambience synchronised to the picture, and does it
  survive at the same quality across the clip?
- **Cost and time.** Credits per generated second, wall-clock time from submit
  to result, and the number of retries a usable clip actually needs.

## How a test is run

1. Freeze the settings before generating: mode, resolution, aspect ratio,
   duration, and the number of reference images or videos.
2. Generate the same prompt at least three times so a single lucky seed does not
   become the conclusion.
3. Keep every result, including failures, for the review window.
4. Record the date, model version or host, and the credit cost shown before
   submission.
5. Write the finding as a specific observation ("text was stable in 2 of 3
   runs") rather than a score.

## What we do not claim

- We do not publish a single benchmark number that ranks all models. Video
  quality is task-dependent, and a fair comparison needs the same prompt, the
  same output settings, and the same evaluation criteria.
- We do not present subjective preference as measurement. Where a judgement is
  aesthetic, the article says so.
- We do not claim a capability the public documentation does not list. If a
  feature is undocumented or provider-specific, the article says that too.

## Pricing and availability

Prices, credit rates, and plan limits are taken from the public pricing pages
and from live checkout screens on the date shown in the article. Because MiniMax
and hosting providers change rates, every pricing page carries a "last updated"
date. The live estimate in the playground is authoritative if a rate changes
between article updates.

## Corrections and updates

If a claim goes stale, or if you can show a test that contradicts one of ours,
email [support@minimaxh3.pro](mailto:support@minimaxh3.pro) with the URL and the
evidence. Corrections are made in place and the update date changes; we do not
silently rewrite a conclusion. Articles are reviewed monthly, and immediately
when MiniMax ships a new model version, changes API access, or changes pricing.
