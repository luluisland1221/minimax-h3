## Who publishes this site

minimaxh3.pro is an independent third-party workspace for generating videos
with MiniMax H3. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or
operated by MiniMax. We do not use official MiniMax branding, and we do not
present ourselves as the official product.

Guides are written by the [MiniMax H3 Editorial Team](/authors/minimax-h3), which
researches and tests the workflows described on this site.

## Independence and disclosure

- We do not accept payment for a review, a ranking, or a comparison placement.
- Any affiliate, referral, or sponsorship relationship is disclosed in the
  article that contains it.
- When we link to a provider, it is because the link answers the reader's
  question, not because of a commercial arrangement.
- We do not run ads on the model-comparison pages.

## Sources and verification

Claims about model capabilities come from the public MiniMax documentation,
recorded at the date shown in the article. Claims about output quality come from
our own tests, described in the [testing methodology](/methodology). Prices are
checked against the live pricing page and the checkout screen.

Where a fact cannot be verified, the article says so rather than guessing. If a
number changes, the article is updated and the date changes with it.

## Use of AI assistance

Drafting, editing, and code examples may be produced with AI assistance. Every
published claim is reviewed by a human editor, and tested results are generated
in the product itself, not written from a model's description of it. AI
assistance is not used to invent test outcomes, quotes, or citations.

## Corrections

If something is wrong, email [support@minimaxh3.pro](mailto:support@minimaxh3.pro)
with the page URL and what is incorrect. We correct errors in place, keep the
update date current, and explain material changes inside affected articles
rather than deleting the old claim.

## Content we do not publish

We do not publish sexual, non-consensual, or deceptive content, and we do not
publish guides for using the model to impersonate real people. Requests to
generate that kind of material are outside the scope of this site.
